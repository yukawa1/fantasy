High S/N: refinement

Above S/N ≈ 45 the data show what a simple model leaves out. fa.refine (the app's Fit step does this for the narrow-line classes) masks tellurics and sky lines, and if χ2ν > 1.2 tries the physical extensions one at a time, keeping one only if ΔBIC < −10 and its new parameters do not end at a limit:

extensiondescribes
calibration polynomial1–3% errors of the flux calibration and dust law
outflow wingbroad bases of the strong lines
Gauss–Hermite narrow linesasymmetric, peaked or flat-topped lines
broad Balmer linesLINER 1.9
