Parameters, limits and ties

Every parameter has a value, limits and a status: free, fixed or tied. Names are component.parameter; patterns with * address several at once.
model.set("narrow.fwhm", value=400, min=100, max=900)
model.fix("continuum.index", -1.7);  model.free("continuum.index")
model.tie("broad2.shift", "broad.shift")
model.tie("narrow.[NII]6548.flux", "narrow.[NII]6583.flux", factor=1/3.05)
model.set("broad*.shift", min=-2000)
model.untie("broad2.shift")
model.set_profile("broad", "lorentzian")      # change the line profile of a component
model.split_lines("narrow", ["[OIII]4959", "[OIII]5007"], "narrow_oiii")   # own shift and width for [O III]

A tie is affine:

p = a · ptarget + b

Amplitudes can be tied only to amplitudes (b = 0), widths and shifts only to widths and shifts; cycles are refused. Limits are given as (start, min, max), e.g. fwhm=(3000, 1000, 9000) km/s.

Parameters at a limit. When a parameter ends at its limit, the limit and not the data sets its value and its error is not meaningful. The summary lists them; widen the limit, fix the parameter or change the model.

After a fit, r.params gives value, error, limits and status; r.at_limit the parameters at a limit; r.unconstrained those without data (e.g. a line outside the fitted range); r.not_measured the widths and shifts of components without flux (their values mean nothing, so r.lines shows NaN); r.unmeasured_fluxes the line fluxes with an error at least as large as the flux.
