FANTASY 2.1.0 / Measurements

Black-hole masses

Single-epoch virial masses from the width of a broad line and a luminosity, with the main calibrations of the literature.
log MBH = a + b log L10n erg s−1 + c log W1000 km s−1

W is the FWHM (or σline) of the summed broad line, L the continuum or broad-line luminosity.

linerecipewidth, luminosityreference
Hβvp06 (default)FWHM, L5100Vestergaard & Peterson 2006
Hβdb20, db20_sigmaFWHM or σ, L(Hβ broad)Dalla Bontà et al. 2020

C IVvp06 (default), shen24FWHM, L1350Vestergaard & Peterson 2006; Shen et al. 2024
r.bh_mass("Hb"); r.bh_mass("Hb", recipe="db20"); r.bh_masses()

The summary chooses a broad line with a measured profile. A dispersion-based mass requires a defined full-profile dispersion. The built-in recipes omit these masses for profiles with damping wings.

The errors are statistical. The recipes scatter by 0.3–0.6 dex around reverberation masses, which is not in the error. Recipes with the broad-line luminosity need no host subtraction.
