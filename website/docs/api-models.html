FANTASY 2.0.0 / API reference

Models

fa.Model(*components, wave_range=None)

Sum of components. Build it from components and combine with +:

model = Model(PowerLaw(), FeII(), Lines("narrow", [...]), Lines("broad", [...]))
model["broad.fwhm"].set(value=4000, min=1500, max=12000)
model.tie("broad.Hg.fwhm", "broad.Hb.fwhm")
model.fix("feii.shift", 0)

Parameter names are component.parameter; set, fix, free and tie also accept shell-style patterns such as "broad.*.flux".

Model.starting_from(result)

A copy of the model whose free parameters start at the values of a fit (result, a FitResult, or a fitted Model), wherever the two have a parameter of the same name; the limits, fixed values and ties stay those of this model. A fit of the copy tries these values as a start besides the data-driven guess and keeps the better solution, so it never ends worse than result. Use it to carry a model worked out on one epoch to the others:

r = fa.fit(spectra[3], model)                       # agree on the model for one epoch
me = fa.fit_epochs(spectra, model.starting_from(r)) # then fit them all from there

fa.presets.hbeta(broad_components=2, feii=True, outflow=True, host=False, heii=True, continuum='powerlaw', extinction=False, coronal=False, broad_profile='gaussian', wave_range=(4150, 5550), balmer_decrement=True)

H-beta region (4150-5550 A): power law, optical FeII, narrow H-gamma, [OIII]4363, H-beta and [OIII]4959,5007 (fixed 1:2.98 ratio), broad H-gamma and H-beta with shared kinematics, narrow [NI]5198,5200, broad HeI4471 and HeII4686.

broad_componentsnumber of broad components (1, 2, 3, ...).
broad_profile'gaussian', 'lorentzian', 'voigt' or 'gauss-hermite'. Compare result.bic between fits to decide how many the data support.
outflowadd a blueshifted [OIII] wing component (default: on; in the calibration on SDSS spectra (calibration/report.md) the BIC preferred it for 60-70% of the quasars).
feiioptical FeII template; its velocity shift is tied to the main broad H-beta component (preferred by the BIC for 72% of the quasars, and the free shift ended at its limit in a quarter of the fits).
hostfit a host galaxy simultaneously: True/'stellar' (stellar population templates with sigma* and v*) or 'pca' (Yip+2004 eigenspectra).
continuum'powerlaw' or 'broken' (slope change at 4600 A).
coronaladd the coronal lines [FeVII]5159 and [FeXIV]5303 (own kinematics), seen in many Seyferts.
balmer_decrementbroad H-gamma as a ratio to H-beta, 0.1-0.5 (case B 0.47), in every broad component, and broad H-delta (its wing enters a window starting below 4250 A) tied to H-gamma by the case-B ratio. False: free H-gamma flux and no H-delta (FANTASY 2.0.0 and earlier).

fa.presets.halpha(broad_components=2, outflow=False, host=False, continuum='powerlaw', extinction=False, hei=True, coronal=False, broad_profile='gaussian', wave_range=(6200, 6900))

H-alpha region (6200-6900 A): power law, narrow [OI]6300,6364, [NII]6548,6583 (1:3.05), H-alpha, HeI6678, [SII]6716,6731, broad H-alpha and broad HeI6678.

  • True: free kinematics (FWHM <= 2000 km/s) and [NII]6583/H-alpha <= 3.

In this window nothing else constrains the wing, which can then mimic structure in the broad line.

  • a FitResult of a fit with an [OIII] outflow (e.g. the hbeta preset

with outflow=True), or a dict {'shift': km/s, 'fwhm': km/s}: the wing has the shift and FWHM of the [OIII] outflow, so it can only be the gas seen in [OIII]. Recommended.

outflowadd an outflow wing to [NII] and H-alpha. Off by default: in the calibration (calibration/report.md) the BIC preferred a free wing for 61% of the quasars, but its width ended at a limit in a quarter of the fits, since nothing in this window constrains it. Prefer the [OIII] kinematics:
heiadd HeI6678, narrow (narrow-line kinematics) and broad (kinematics of the main broad H-alpha component, so it cannot absorb the red wing of H-alpha).
coronaladd the coronal line [FeX]6375 (own kinematics).

fa.presets.optical(broad_components=2, feii=True, outflow=True, host=False, continuum='broken', balmer=True, extinction=False, coronal=False, oi6046=False, red=False, broad_profile='gaussian', wave_range=None, physical=False, slope=-1.7, broad_fwhm_max=None, balmer_decrement=True)

Full optical range (3700-6900 A): broken power law, Balmer continuum and high-order Balmer lines, FeII, narrow lines from [OII]3727 to [SII]6731 (including H8, [NeIII]3968 + H-epsilon, [SII]4069,4076, [NI]5198,5200 and HeI6678), broad H-delta/H-gamma/H-beta (shared kinematics), broad H-alpha (own kinematics) and broad HeI4471, HeII4686, HeI5876, HeI6678 (shared kinematics).

  • one AGN power law over the whole range with slope alpha_lambda =

slope (default -1.7, Selsing et al. 2016; QSFit fixes it for z < 0.6, where it is degenerate with the host). slope=None fits it within the accretion-disc range -2.33 ... -1.0. No broken power law: the slope change near 5000 A is mostly host-galaxy light;

  • a stellar-population host galaxy (host=True unless given);
  • the velocity-broadened Balmer continuum and high-order Balmer lines,

with a window starting at 3550 A so that the Balmer edge is covered;

  • with outflow=True an [NII]/H-alpha wing with the kinematics of the

[OIII] outflow. Check result.balmer_decrement(): broad H-alpha/H-beta near 3 (Dong et al. 2008) indicates a consistent decomposition.

outflowan [OIII]/[NeIII] wing (default: on; the BIC preferred it for 87% of the quasars in the calibration on SDSS spectra (calibration/report.md)).
hostalso see physical. For quasars at z < 0.35 whose host fraction at 5100 A exceeds 0.5 (fa.classify -> 'type1_host'), a stellar host lowered the median reduced chi2 from 1.33 to 1.01 and the BIC for 85% of them; below a host fraction of 0.3 it did not help.
coronaladd the coronal lines [FeVII]3759,5159,5721,6087, [FeXIV]5303 and [FeX]6375 (shared kinematics of their own).
oi6046add broad OI6046 (strong in objects with strong FeII).
redextend the fit to 7500 A (if the spectrum covers it) with narrow HeI7065, [ArIII]7136 and [OII]7320,7330 and broad HeI7065.
physicalphysically motivated decomposition (after QSFit, Calderone et al. 2017, and the references in the documentation):
slopeAGN power-law slope for physical=True (None: fit it).
broad_fwhm_maxupper FWHM limit of the broad components, km/s (default 15000, see BROAD_FWHM_MAX).
balmer_decrementbroad H-gamma and H-delta as ratios to H-beta in every broad component (H-gamma/H-beta 0.1-0.5, H-delta tied to H-gamma by the case-B ratio); False: free fluxes.
extinctionTrue (Fitzpatrick 1999) or 'calzetti': fit an intrinsic E(B-V) of the AGN; fluxes and luminosities are then dereddened.
continuum'broken' (default; slope change at 4600 A), 'broken_free' (break wavelength fitted within 4000-6000 A) or 'powerlaw'. A single power law cannot follow the curvature of the continuum over this wide range. The continuum shape is the main systematic of this preset: compare several options (and host=True) with fa.compare.
balmeradd the Balmer continuum and high-order Balmer lines, which shape the spectrum below ~4000 A.
hostfit a host galaxy simultaneously: True/'stellar' (stellar population templates with sigma* and v*, cannot become negative) or 'pca' (Yip+2004 eigenspectra; can come out negative, the summary warns).

fa.presets.mgii(broad_components=1, feii=True, balmer_continuum=True, narrow=False, broad_profile='gaussian', wave_range=(2650, 3050))

MgII region (2650-3050 A): power law, Balmer continuum, UV FeII and broad MgII2798 (doublet blend).

feiiTrue or 'uv': the UV Fe II model of Kovacevic-Dojcinovic & Popovic (2015), with which this preset was calibrated; 'uv_extended': the UV model built as the optical one (2200-3500 A, see fantasy_agn.FeII; widen wave_range to use its range); False: none.
narrowadd a narrow MgII component. Off by default: in the calibration on 200 SDSS quasars (calibration/report.md) its width ended at the upper limit in half of the fits (it became part of the broad line) and the BIC preferred the model without it for 77%. One broad component was preferred for 80%.

fa.presets.civ(broad_components=2, broad_profile='gaussian', wave_range=(1450, 1720))

CIV region (1450-1720 A): power law, broad CIV1549 (two components by default: preferred by the BIC for 95% of 150 SDSS quasars in the calibration), HeII1640 and OIII]1663 with the shift of the main broad CIV component (a free shift ended at its -3000 km/s limit in half of the fits, absorbing the red shelf of CIV).

Absorption lines and troughs are common here and dominate chi2: fit them with fa.fit_absorbed(spec, fa.presets.civ()), or mask narrow ones with spec.mask_absorption().

fa.presets.type2(outflow=True, host=True, featureless=True, coronal=False, extinction=False, wave_range=(3700, 6900))

Type 2 AGN (Seyfert 2, LINER, composite): stellar-population host (with its Balmer absorption, which matters for H-beta), an optional weak featureless AGN continuum (power law, alpha_lambda = -1.7: scattered light), narrow lines from [OII]3727 to [SII]6731 with shared kinematics and an outflow wing on H-beta, [OIII], [NII] and H-alpha. No broad lines.

featurelessadd the featureless power-law continuum.
coronaladd coronal lines (see optical).
extinctionfit an intrinsic E(B-V) of the emission (not the host).

fa.presets.galaxy(host=True, outflow=False, extinction=False, wave_range=(3700, 6900))

Star-forming, retired or passive galaxy: stellar populations (sigma*, v*, age, metallicity) and narrow emission lines with shared kinematics, no AGN continuum. outflow=True adds a broad wing to the strong lines (galactic winds).

fa.presets.seyfert2(host=True, outflow=True, two_systems=True, featureless=True, coronal=False, red=True, wave_range=None, narrow_profile='gaussian', calibration=0)

Seyfert 2 galaxy: stellar populations with dust (E-MILES; host='bc03' for BC03), a weak featureless continuum (scattered AGN light, power law with alpha_lambda = -1.7), narrow lines from [OII]3727 to [OII]7330 in two kinematic systems (high ionisation: [NeIII], [OIII], HeII, [ArIII]; low ionisation: Balmer, [NII], [SII], [OI], ...), and an outflow wing on [NeIII], H-beta, [OIII], H-alpha, [NII] and [SII]. Line fluxes are free: the Balmer decrement measures the reddening afterwards.

two_systemsseparate kinematics for high- and low-ionisation lines.
redextend the fit to 7500 A (HeI 7065, [ArIII] 7136, [OII] 7320, 7330).
coronaladd coronal lines ([FeVII], [FeX], [FeXIV]).
narrow_profile'gaussian' or 'gauss-hermite' (h3, h4: asymmetric and peaked/flat-topped profiles, for spectra of high S/N; see fantasy_agn.refine).
calibrationdegree of a smooth multiplicative correction (fantasy_agn.Calibration; 0 = none, ~10 over 3700-7500 A) for flux-calibration and dust-law errors, needed at S/N > ~40.

fa.presets.composite(host=True, outflow=False, two_systems=True, featureless=True, red=True, wave_range=None, narrow_profile='gaussian', calibration=0)

Composite galaxy (between the Kauffmann 2003 and Kewley 2001 lines): star formation and an AGN. Like seyfert2 (stellar populations with dust, weak featureless continuum, two narrow-line systems). No outflow wing by default: in the calibration on 100 SDSS composites the BIC preferred the model without it for 76%, and its width ended at the 2500 km/s limit in half of the fits (it absorbed template mismatch, not gas).

fa.presets.liner(host=True, broad=False, outflow=False, featureless=True, red=True, wave_range=None, narrow_profile='gaussian', calibration=0)

LINER: a dominant old stellar population with dust, a weak featureless continuum and one narrow-line system that may be broad (FWHM up to 1500 km/s). The Balmer lines are weak against the stellar absorption, so their fluxes stay free. broad=True adds a broad H-beta/H-alpha (LINER 1.9); outflow=True an outflow wing.

fa.presets.starforming(host='bc03', outflow=False, extinction=True, wolf_rayet=False, red=True, wave_range=None, narrow_profile='gaussian', calibration=0)

Star-forming galaxy: young and old stellar populations with dust (BC03 from 1 Myr; E-MILES starts at 63 Myr and cannot follow young continua), narrow lines including the high-order Balmer series, He I, the auroral lines [OIII]4363, [NII]5755, [SIII]6312 and [OII]7320,7330 (electron temperatures), Balmer lines tied to Case B so that the Balmer decrement gives the gas extinction E(B-V) (extinction). outflow=True adds a broad wing on the strong lines (galactic winds); off by default, since in the calibration on 150 SDSS star-forming galaxies the BIC preferred the model without it for 84%.

wolf_rayetadd the Wolf-Rayet bumps at 4650 and 5808 A.
narrow_profile'gaussian' or 'gauss-hermite' (see seyfert2).
calibrationdegree of the smooth multiplicative correction (see seyfert2).

fa.presets.passive(host=True, red=True, wave_range=None, calibration=0)

Passive or retired galaxy (EW(H-alpha) < 3 A): stellar populations with dust for sigma*, v*, age and metallicity, plus weak narrow lines with limited widths (so that they cannot absorb template mismatch). The retired galaxies of the WHAN diagram are ionised by old stars.

fa.presets.tde(host=True, bowen=True, helium=True, narrow=True, broad_profile='gaussian', feii=False, coronal=False, temperature=(20000.0, 5000.0, 80000.0), wave_range=(3800, 7000), broad_fwhm=None)

Tidal disruption event (TDE) in the optical: a hot blackbody continuum (T 1-5e4 K) plus the host galaxy (stellar populations with dust, usually quiescent or post-starburst), broad H-delta/H-gamma/H-beta (shared kinematics) and H-alpha (own kinematics), broad He II 4686 with the Bowen fluorescence lines N III 4640 (and N III 4100, O III 3760 with bowen=True where they are in the range) sharing its kinematics, broad He I 5876, 6678, and weak narrow host-galaxy lines. Classes of van Velzen et al. (2021): TDE-H (Balmer), TDE-H+He (Balmer + He II / N III), TDE-He; Bowen lines: Blagorodnova et al. (2019), Leloudas et al. (2019).

TDE lines are broad (FWHM up to ~2e4 km/s) and often have extended wings (electron scattering, Roth & Kasen 2018; or a stratified emitting region): try broad_profile='lorentzian' or 'stratified'.

temperature(start, min, max) in K; fix it (e.g. from UV photometry) with model.fix('continuum.temperature', T) -- the optical alone barely constrains it.
bowenTrue (default): N III 4640 with He II; 'all': also N III 4100 (blended with H-delta) and O III 3760 (at the blue edge, where a broad line can act as continuum); False: He II only.
heliumadd He II 4686 (and He II 5412) and He I 5876, 6678.
feiiadd the optical Fe II template (strong in some nuclear transients, e.g. Bowen fluorescence flares; Baldini et al. 2025).
coronaladd the coronal iron lines ([Fe VII], [Fe X], [Fe XIV]; extreme coronal line emitters).
broad_fwhm(start, min, max) FWHM of the broad lines in km/s.

fa.presets.bff(host=True, broad_profile='gaussian', temperature=(20000.0, 5000.0, 80000.0), wave_range=(3800, 7000))

Bowen fluorescence flare / extreme coronal line emitter: a nuclear transient with relatively narrow broad Balmer lines (FWHM ~1500 km/s), strong He II 4686 with the Bowen lines N III 4640, 4100 and O III 3760, Fe II and coronal iron lines (Trakhtenbrot et al. 2019; Baldini et al. 2025, A&A 701, A224). The tde model with Fe II and coronal lines and broad lines from 1500 km/s.

fa.presets.auto(spectrum, **options)

The most suitable preset for the spectrum's rest-frame coverage.

fa.presets.variants(name='hbeta', broad_components=(1, 2, 3), outflow=(False, True), broad_profile=('gaussian',), host=None, **options)

Several decompositions of one preset for fa.compare(): every combination of the number of broad components, the broad-line profile and with/without an outflow wing (where the preset has one). Other options (e.g. host=True) apply to all. For H-alpha, outflow=(False, hbeta_result) ties the wing to the [OIII] outflow.

broad_profileprofile name or several, e.g. ('gaussian', 'gauss-hermite'): Gauss-Hermite components can follow asymmetric broad lines (bumps, shoulders) with fewer components.
hoste.g. (False, True): also vary the stellar host (for quasars at low redshift the host is a main systematic of L5100 and the widths).

fa.Lines(name, lines, fwhm=(500, 50, 2000), shift=(0, -1000, 1000), ratios=None, tie_kinematics=True, absorption=False, flux_max=inf, role=None, profile='gaussian', h3=(0, -0.3, 0.3), h4=(0, -0.3, 0.3), fwhm_l=None, max_ratios=None, span=(0.6, 0.0, 2.5), gamma=(2.5, 0.0, 5.0), line_ratios=None)

A group of Gaussian emission lines, e.g. all narrow lines or all broad lines.

Parameters are the integrated flux of each line (<name>.<line>.flux) and, when tie_kinematics is True (default), one shared velocity shift (<name>.shift, km/s) and FWHM (<name>.fwhm, km/s). With tie_kinematics=False every line has its own shift and FWHM.

  • 'gaussian' (default): fwhm is the intrinsic FWHM.
  • 'lorentzian': fwhm is the Lorentzian FWHM.
  • 'voigt': fwhm is the Gaussian FWHM and fwhm_l the Lorentzian FWHM.
  • 'gauss-hermite': Gaussian core fwhm with skewness h3 and

kurtosis h4 (van der Marel & Franx 1993); the flux does not depend on h3 and h4.

  • 'stratified': a radially stratified, virialised BLR: Gaussians of FWHM

fwhm (r/r_out)^(-1/2) summed from r_in to r_out = 10^span r_in with dL/d ln r ~ r^(3 - gamma). fwhm is the width at the outer edge (the core), fwhm 10^(span/2) the width at the inner edge; the wings fall roughly as a power law, v^-2 (Lorentzian-like) for gamma ~ 3. span = 0 is a single Gaussian. For non-Gaussian profiles, result.measure(line) gives the actual FWHM, line dispersion and centroid of the profile.

namecomponent name, e.g. 'narrow', 'broad'.
linesline names from the library (e.g. 'Hb', '[OIII]5007') or (name, vacuum wavelength) pairs.
fwhminitial FWHM or (min, max) or (initial, min, max), km/s.
shiftinitial shift or (min, max) or (initial, min, max), km/s.
ratiosdict {line: (reference line, flux ratio)} fixing flux ratios, e.g. {'[OIII]4959': ('[OIII]5007', 1/2.98)}.
max_ratiosdict {line: (reference line, maximum ratio)} keeping flux(line) <= ratio * flux(reference), e.g. {'[NII]6583': ('Ha', 3)}. The reference line is then parametrised by its flux in excess of flux(line) / ratio (<name>.<reference>.excess, >= 0), which keeps the problem linear in the fluxes with simple bounds.
line_ratiosdict {line: (reference line, (start, min, max))} making the flux ratio line/reference a free parameter within physical limits (<name>.<line>.ratio) instead of a free flux, e.g. {'Hg': ('Hb', (0.35, 0.1, 0.5))} keeps the Balmer decrement H-gamma/H-beta between 0.1 and 0.5 (case B: 0.47). The reference keeps its flux parameter.
absorptionif True, fluxes are negative (absorption lines).
role'narrow', 'broad', 'outflow' or None (guessed from the name, else from the initial FWHM). Used to pick components for black-hole masses and plots.
profileline shape:
h3, h4, fwhm_llimits of the extra shape parameters, as for fwhm.

fa.PowerLaw(name='continuum', pivot=5100.0, index=(-1.5, -5, 3))

f = amp * (x / pivot) ** index; amp is the flux density at pivot.

fa.BrokenPowerLaw(name='continuum', pivot=5100.0, index_blue=(-1.5, -5, 3), index_red=(-1.5, -5, 3), break_wave=None)

Power law with different indices blueward and redward of a break.

pivotbreak wavelength (A), fixed; amp is the flux density there.
break_wave(initial, min, max) to fit the break wavelength instead (parameter break); then pivot is ignored and amp is the flux density at the fitted break.

fa.Polynomial(name='poly', order=2, pivot=5100.0, scale=1000.0)

Polynomial in (x - pivot) / scale; all coefficients are linear.

fa.FeII(name='feii', kind='optical', fwhm=(2000, 1000, 6000), shift=(0, -1500, 1500), amp_max=inf)

FeII emission template: lines grouped in multiplets with relative intensities fixed within each multiplet and a common shift and FWHM. kind='optical' uses the Kovacevic et al. (2010) / Shapovalova et al. (2012) model (3700-10900 A); kind='uv' the UV model around Mg II of Kovacevic-Dojcinovic & Popovic (2015, 2690-3005 A: multiplets UV 60-63, 78 and the I Zw 1 lines); kind='uv_extended' a UV model built as the optical one (2200-3500 A: lines grouped by lower term, a4D split into UV 60-63 as in 2015, intensities at 10^4 K from NIST atomic data, and the I Zw 1 lines).

fa.BalmerContinuum(name='balmer_cont', temperature=15000.0, tau=1.0, fix_shape=True, fwhm=5000.0, fix_fwhm=True)

Balmer continuum (Grandi 1982; Dietrich et al. 2002): optically thick Planck function below the Balmer edge (3646 A). amp is the flux density just blueward of the edge (before broadening). Temperature and optical depth are fixed by default (15000 K, tau=1), as is common practice. The edge is smeared by the velocities of the emitting gas: the shape is convolved with a Gaussian of FWHM fwhm (km/s; default 5000, fixed, as in QSFit, Calderone et al. 2017); fwhm=0 gives a sharp edge.

fa.HighOrderBalmer(name='balmer_lines', fwhm=(3000, 1000, 10000), shift=(0, -1500, 1500), max_wavelength=4000.0)

High-order Balmer lines blending towards the Balmer edge (3646 A), with fixed relative intensities and one common amplitude, shift and FWHM.

Only lines blueward of max_wavelength are included. The default (4000 A) starts at H-epsilon (3970 A), so that H-gamma and H-delta, which are usually fitted as separate lines, are not counted twice. Do not also fit H-epsilon separately unless you lower max_wavelength.

fa.StellarPopulation(name='host', templates='emiles', ages=None, metals=(-0.71, 0.0, 0.22), velocity=(0, -600, 600), sigma=(150, 30, 450), reddening=False, ebv=(0.1, 0.0, 1.5), dv=20.0)

Host galaxy as a non-negative combination of stellar population templates with stellar velocity velocity and dispersion sigma (km/s).

Template weights are named <name>.w00, <name>.w01, ...; the grid attribute lists the age and metallicity of each. After a fit, result.host_properties() reports sigma*, v*, the host fraction and the light-weighted age and metallicity with uncertainties.

templatestemplate set ('emiles' by default; see fantasy_agn.templates).
agesages in Gyr to use (nearest available), default every second age of the grid.
metalsmetallicities [M/H] to use, default (-0.71, 0, +0.22).
velocity, sigmainitial value and limits, (value, min, max) in km/s.
reddeningfit a dust screen E(B-V) (Fitzpatrick 1999) on the host.
dvvelocity step of the internal log-wavelength grid (km/s).

fa.HostGalaxy(name='host', n_components=5)

Host galaxy as a linear combination of the SDSS galaxy eigenspectra of Yip et al. (2004), with their emission lines interpolated over. Fitting it together with the AGN components propagates the host uncertainty into all other parameters. Coefficients are unbounded (eigenspectra can enter with either sign); check that the resulting host spectrum is positive.

fa.Extinction(name='extinction', law='F99', ebv=(0.1, 0.0, 2.0), r_v=None)

Intrinsic dust screen on the AGN: every component except the host galaxy is multiplied by 10**(-0.4 E(B-V) k(lambda)). Fitted line fluxes, continuum fluxes and luminosities are then the dereddened (intrinsic) values, with the extinction uncertainty included in their errors; plots show the observed (reddened) components.

E(B-V) is degenerate with the slope of the power law over narrow wavelength ranges; it is best constrained over a wide range or with fixed line ratios (e.g. a tied narrow Balmer decrement).

law'F99' (Fitzpatrick 1999, Milky Way, R_V = 3.1) or 'calzetti' (Calzetti et al. 2000, R_V = 4.05).
ebvinitial value and limits of E(B-V), mag.
