Classification

fa.classify(spec) decides what kind of object a spectrum shows, before a model is chosen, with three quick fits.
  1. Starlight. E-MILES stellar populations with dust plus a weak AGN power law, emission lines masked: the host fraction at 5100 Å and σ★, and the stellar Balmer absorption under Hβ removed before line ratios are measured.
  2. Broad lines. The starlight-subtracted spectrum with narrow lines only, and with broad Hβ/Hα added. A broad-line region needs ΔBIC > 25, S/N > 5, EW > 30 Å and 1200 < FWHM < 12 000 km/s; weaker candidates are reported as type 1.8/1.9.
  3. Narrow-line diagnostics. BPT ([N II], [S II], [O I]) and WHAN diagrams.

A UV label says which line can be fitted, not that the object has broad lines: nothing tests that. Look at the width of the line and at the catalogs.

Catalogs

Look up in step 1 (Read), below the list of spectra, or fa.lookup_catalogs(files), shows a sky image and searches around the sky position in SIMBAD, NED and Milliquas (name, type, redshift), AllWISE, FIRST and NVSS, and XMM, Chandra and 2RXS. Only the coordinates are sent, and only when you press the button. It warns when a catalog lists a quasar classified here as passive or star-forming, a star, a galaxy fitted with broad lines, a different redshift, or W1−W2 ≥ 0.8 (Stern et al. 2012) for a class without an AGN. A catalog redshift often comes from the same spectrum, so agreement is not always an independent test. A service that cannot be reached is named; its results are missing, not negative.

c.uncertain is set when a ratio is within its error of a boundary or a diagnostic is missing; c.plot() and c.plot_steps() show the diagrams and the steps. The diagnostics need Hα: up to z ≈ 0.4 for SDSS and DESI.
