Epochs and reverberation

fa.epoch_table(results, lines=None, continuum_at=None, n_jobs=-1)

One row per epoch: the flux, FWHM, EW, line dispersion and shift of each broad line, the continuum and log L, R_FeII and the total [OIII]5007 flux, each with its error.

resultsa MultiEpochResult or a list of FitResults of the same model (epochs fitted one by one).
linesbroad lines to measure (default: all of them).
continuum_atwavelength of the continuum (default: the pivot of the continuum component).
n_jobsprocesses (default: all cores but one; 1 = serial).

fa.epoch_correlations(table, columns=None)

Spearman rank correlation (rho, p) and Pearson r for every pair of quantities of an epoch table (epochs with a missing value are left out of that pair), strongest first; trivial marks pairs that are the same number twice (the continuum and its luminosity, widths tied in the model), listed last.

fa.EpochSet(fits)

Epochs fitted one by one (fa.fit_many) seen together, with the tools of a joint fit that need only the epochs: light_curve, rms_spectrum, reverberation (lags, rms widths, mass) and plot_baldwin:

fits = fa.fit_many(spectra, model)
me = fa.EpochSet(fits)
rm = me.reverberation("Hb")

Unlike fa.fit_epochs nothing is shared and the epochs are not intercalibrated on the narrow lines: the flux calibration of each spectrum is taken as it is. Epochs whose fit failed are left out.

fa.rm.component_reverberation(me, line='Hb', continuum=None, components=None, feii_band=(4434.0, 4684.0), continuum_at=None, lag_range=None, dt=1.0, n_pass=3, alias=True, seed=0)

Spectro-temporal reverberation: the lag and the response of every spectral component at once, from the multi-epoch fit with its full error structure.

Each epoch gives a vector of component fluxes (the AGN continuum, each broad component of line, FeII in feii_band) with their joint covariance over all epochs: within an epoch the decomposition and calibration errors (the components trade flux with each other), between epochs the errors of shared parameters. Every component is modelled as

F_c(t) = m_c + a_c x(t - tau_c) + intrinsic noise (variance j_c^2)

with x(t) the continuum variation, a damped random walk conditioned on the photometric continuum (or fitted to the spectroscopic one). The response a_c may be zero: the data decide which components reverberate (with which lag) and which vary only through the decomposition. The lags come from the profile likelihood of each tau_c (the other lags at their best values, iterated n_pass times), weighted for the overlap of the light curves. Also returned: the component light curves with the decomposition noise removed (the conditional mean given the model).

continuum(mjd, flux, error) of a photometric continuum, or None.
componentsbroad components to use (default: all with the line).
returnsComponentReverberation.

fa.rm.lag_significance(lag)

How clearly a lag differs from zero: (p, sigma). p is the fraction of the lag distribution (all Monte Carlo or posterior samples, aliases included) on the side of zero where the lag is, sigma the one-sided Gaussian equivalent (2 sigma: 97.7% of the distribution on that side). When every sample is on that side, sigma is the lower limit the number of samples allows (its value is then marked by p = 1). (nan, nan) without samples.

MultiEpochResult.shared

no description

MultiEpochResult.scales

Flux calibration factor of each epoch relative to the reference epoch.

MultiEpochResult.offsets

Wavelength offset of each epoch (km/s, with wavelength_offsets) relative to the reference epoch; None if they were not fitted.

MultiEpochResult.broadening

Extra broadening of each epoch (FWHM in km/s added in quadrature, negative: sharper; with epoch_broadening) relative to the reference epoch, and the resolution it gives; None if not fitted.

MultiEpochResult.redshift(parameter='narrow.shift')

Redshift of the object from the joint fit: the redshift the reference epoch was moved to the rest frame with, corrected by the shared velocity parameter (default: the shift of the narrow lines, which all epochs measure together). Returns (z, error); the error is that of the fit only, without the wavelength calibration of the reference spectrum (a few km/s for SDSS, 1e-5 in z). Use it to prepare the spectra again with one redshift, or as the systemic redshift.

MultiEpochResult.light_curve(lines=None, continuum_at=None)

Table with, per epoch, the total broad flux and FWHM of each broad line (summed over broad components) and the continuum flux density.

MultiEpochResult.window_continuum(windows=None, subtract_lines=True, min_coverage=0.8)

Continuum light curves measured in rest-frame windows: per epoch, the mean flux density of the intercalibrated spectrum in each window, minus the fitted emission lines and Fe II (subtract_lines), with the error of the mean from the pixel errors. Unlike light_curve, which evaluates the fitted AGN continuum at one wavelength, this is measured on the data, as in reverberation-mapping campaigns; it includes the host starlight (constant: it dilutes the variability, not the lag).

:data:CONTINUUM_WINDOWS (Shen et al. 2011) inside the fitted range

windowslist of (lo, hi) in Å; default: the windows of
min_coverageshare of a window's pixels an epoch must have, else NaN
sDataFrame with mjd and win{lo}_{hi}, win{lo}_{hi}_err

MultiEpochResult.reverberation(line='Hb', continuum=None, lag_range=None, dt=None, n_mc=1000, f_virial=4.47, mjd_range=None, detrend=False, methods=('iccf', 'vn', 'drw'), primary='iccf', width=0.0, seed=0)

Lag of the broad line behind the continuum, its width in the rms spectrum and the reverberation mass; returns a fantasy_agn.rm.Reverberation (print it, .table(), .plot()).

Lag methods (methods; primary gives the adopted lag): 'iccf' (interpolated cross-correlation, FR/RSS errors), 'vn' (von Neumann estimator), 'zdcf' (z-transformed discrete correlation function, Alexander 1997) and 'drw' (damped-random-walk model with the correlated line and continuum errors of each epoch's fit, see fantasy_agn.rm.gp_lag). All keep the primary peak of the lag distribution weighted for the light-curve overlap (seasonal aliases).

continuumNone: the fitted AGN continuum light curve of these epochs; or (mjd, flux, error) arrays, e.g. photometry (better sampled).
mjd_range(min, max): only epochs (and continuum points) in this window, e.g. one observing season; lags need dense sampling.
lag_range(min, max) observed-frame lag to search, days (default: +-5x the lag expected from L5100 and the R-L relation, >= 60 d).
detrendsubtract a linear trend from both light curves (Welsh 1999): slow variations otherwise dominate the cross-correlation.

MultiEpochResult.rms_spectrum(line='Hb', mjd_range=None, **kw)

Mean and rms spectrum of a broad line (see fantasy_agn.rm.rms_spectrum).
