FANTASY 2.0.0 / Modelling

Absorption troughs

Intervening absorbers and the troughs of BAL and mini-BAL quasars cut into C IV and Mg II and dominate χ2. FANTASY can find and fit them, so that the emission components describe the unabsorbed spectrum.

Every component is multiplied by the transmission

T(λ) = exp[ −Σk τk exp(−(v − vk)22σk2) ]

Fit absorption troughs (Fit step) fits the model, finds troughs in the residuals, adds them and refits while the BIC improves. Troughs are kept narrower than 6000 km/s so that a shallow “absorber” cannot trade flux with a too-broad emission line.

r = fa.fit_absorbed(spec, fa.presets.civ())
r.absorbers; r.no_absorbers
model.add(fa.Absorption([(1510.5, 1200), (1547.0, 300)]))   # by hand: (wavelength, FWHM)
For narrow absorbers only, masking is simpler: spec.mask_absorption() (Prepare).
