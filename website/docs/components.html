FANTASY 2.1.0 / Modelling

Components

Every component returns its flux on the wavelength grid together with exact derivatives with respect to its parameters. Amplitudes (fluxes) enter linearly; widths, shifts and shapes are nonlinear.

Emission lines: fa.Lines

A group of lines with shared kinematics (or one shift and width per line with tie_kinematics=False). Parameters: the integrated flux of each line <name>.<line>.flux, the velocity shift <name>.shift and FWHM <name>.fwhm (km/s). The line centre and Gaussian width are

λc = λ0(1 + vc),    σλ = λc √(FWHM2/8 ln 2 + σinst2)c

so fitted widths are intrinsic: the instrumental resolution σinst of the spectrum is added in quadrature (exactly for Gaussian, Lorentzian and Voigt profiles). Every profile integrates to one, so the flux parameter is the line flux. Each pixel gets the mean of the profile over its width, not its value at the pixel centre (exactly for Gaussians, by Simpson's rule for the other profiles): point values make lines a few pixels wide look wider, by 7% at FWHM 120 km/s and 3% at 200 km/s on SDSS (69 km/s pixels) with a 150 km/s resolution. FANTASY_PIXEL=0 returns to point values.

Options: fixed ratios ratios={"[OIII]4959": ("[OIII]5007", 1/2.98)}, upper limits max_ratios={"[NII]6583": ("Ha", 3)}, absorption=True for absorption lines, custom lines as ("name", vacuum_wavelength). The library of lines is in Reference → Line list; the FANTASY 1 lists are sets (fa.line_set("coronal"), fa.LINE_SETS) and a CSV list of your own is read by fa.read_line_list(path).

The Balmer continuum (Grandi 1982; Dietrich et al. 2002) is an optically thick Planck function below the edge λBE = 3646 Å, smeared by the gas velocities (Gaussian of FWHM 5000 km/s, as QSFit):

Templates

FeII
Fe II lines in multiplet groups with fixed relative intensities, a free amplitude per group and one shift and FWHM (Fe II templates).
HighOrderBalmer
Hε and above blending into the Balmer edge, fixed relative intensities, one amplitude.
StellarPopulation
the host as a non-negative sum of stellar population templates (E-MILES default; BC03, FSPS, XSL) convolved to a stellar velocity v★ and dispersion σ★, optionally with a dust screen.
HostGalaxy
the host as a combination of the SDSS galaxy eigenspectra of Yip et al. (2004).
