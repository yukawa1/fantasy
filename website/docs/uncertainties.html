FANTASY 2.1.0 / Fitting

Uncertainties

Three ways to the errors of the parameters, and one rule for everything derived from them: propagate the full covariance or the samples.

Covariance (default)

C = (JTWJ)−1 · max(1, χ2ν),    W = diag(1/σi2)

J is the exact Jacobian at the best fit. When χ2ν > 1 the errors are inflated by √χ2ν (scale_errors=False switches this off): the error spectrum is then assumed underestimated.

Monte Carlo

errors="mc": the spectrum is perturbed by its errors n_mc times (100) and refitted in parallel; the spread of the fits gives the errors. More reliable for weak or blended lines and parameters near limits.

Posterior (HMC)

errors="mcmc": Hamiltonian Monte Carlo with flat priors within the limits; 4 chains, 300 warm-up and 600 samples each. r.mcmc_info gives the acceptance, split-R̂ and effective sample size; the app shows the corner plot. All free parameters are sampled, also those at a limit, and the chains run in parallel. Chains that have not converged are run once more with a 3× longer warm-up and twice the trajectory (r.mcmc_info["attempts"]). r.upper_limit("broad.Hb.flux") gives the 95% limit on an undetected line; it assumes flat priors, so it depends on the parameter limits.

Derived quantities

The FWHM of a multi-component line, an EW, a luminosity or a black-hole mass is a function g(θ) of all parameters. Its error comes from the samples when there are any, otherwise from linear propagation of the full covariance,

σg2 = ∇gT C ∇g,

so correlations between components (a broad wing against the continuum) are included: r.propagate(func).

These are statistical errors. How a spectrum is decomposed often matters more: see Model comparison.
