FANTASY 2.0.0 / Fitting

Model comparison

Broad-line widths, fluxes and luminosities often depend more on the decomposition than on the noise. FANTASY compares decompositions with the Bayesian information criterion and turns their spread into a systematic error.
BIC = χ2 + k ln N,     AIC = χ2 + 2k

k is the number of free parameters (tied and fixed ones excluded) and N the number of fitted pixels. A difference ΔBIC < −6 strongly favours the model with the lower value; FANTASY uses −10 to accept a refinement and −25 to detect a broad-line region.

models = fa.presets.variants("hbeta", host=(False, True))
cmp = fa.compare(spec, models)       # fitted in parallel
cmp.summary()                        # value, statistical, systematic and total error
r = cmp.result                       # the best (lowest BIC) fit

The systematic error is the spread over the acceptable decompositions (χ2ν within 10% of the best). Example (SDSS quasar, 12 decompositions): Hβ FWHM 4522 ± 56 (stat) ± 52 (model) km/s, but the line dispersion 3524 ± 143 ± 325 km/s and log L5100 44.854 ± 0.003 ± 0.039.
