fa.predictive_check(spectrum, models, blocks, reference=None, wave_range=None, scale_noise=True, n_jobs=-1, **fit_options)

Predict withheld blocks of a spectrum with several models and score the predictions.

Every model is fitted once per block with that block masked; the block is then predicted with the Gaussian predictive density N(model, noise + J C J^T), where C is the covariance of the training fit, so a confident but wrong prediction is penalized. A model whose prediction of a block needs a parameter constrained only by the block (e.g. a free amplitude of a group that lies inside it) cannot predict it: the row is flagged and the block is left out of that model's scores; models are compared on the blocks both predict. The gain over the baseline shows that a model transfers information between blocks; that the transfer follows the proposed link is tested by link_test (a model can beat an empty baseline even when its link is violated).

modelsdict {name: Model}; include the baseline and plausible alternatives (a flexible empirical component, a continuum correction, another mechanism).
blocksdict {name: [(lo, hi), ...]} or a list of (lo, hi): the withheld regions. They should test a physical link (features that the proposed physics connects), not interpolation inside a line.
referencename of the baseline model for PredictiveCheck.gain.
wave_rangefitted range (default: each model's own range, else the whole spectrum).
scale_noisescale the noise of the test pixels by the error scale of the training fit (sqrt of its reduced chi2 when > 1), as the fit scales its own errors.
fit_optionspassed to fantasy_agn.fit.
returnsPredictiveCheck

fa.link_test(spectrum, linked, free, n_sim=0, seed=None, n_jobs=-1, **fit_options)

Cost of the links that give a component its physical meaning: T = chi2(linked) - chi2(free), where free is the same model with the links released (e.g. untied group amplitudes or ratios).

The asymptotic p-value uses chi2 with dof = difference in free parameters; it is unreliable when a released parameter sits at a bound (an amplitude at zero) or is undefined there. With n_sim > 0 the p-value is calibrated by a parametric bootstrap: spectra simulated from the linked fit with its noise, refitted with both models.

returnsdict with T, dof, p_chi2, p_boot (NaN without simulations), the simulated T and the two fits.

fa.profile_likelihood(spectrum, model, parameter, values, n_jobs=-1, **fit_options)

chi2 profile of one parameter: the model is refitted with parameter fixed at each value (all other parameters free; values sorted). Returns a DataFrame (value, chi2, dchi2); .attrs holds the best grid value and the 1-sigma interval (dchi2 = 1, interpolated between grid points). at_grid_edge is True when dchi2 stays below 1 up to an end of the grid: the parameter is not measured on that side.

fa.calibrate(spectrum, null, models, blocks, target, n_sim=50, alpha=0.01, seed=None, reference=None, n_jobs=-1, **fit_options)

False-positive calibration of the specificity of target: spectra are simulated from the fit of the baseline model null (models[null]) with the noise of the spectrum, and the whole predictive check is repeated on each. Returns the simulated specificities, the threshold exceeded by a fraction alpha of them and the fraction of simulations with a positive specificity. The calibration covers only the noise and the models simulated; add simulations with continuum or template mismatch for those cases.
