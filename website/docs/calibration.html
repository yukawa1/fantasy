How the presets were calibrated

The defaults come from fits of 1500 SDSS DR17 spectra in nine groups; starting values and limits were set on 70% of each group and the changes tested on the other 30%.
changewhy (held-out 30%)
[O III] outflow wing in optical and hbetaBIC better for 82% / 66%; χ2ν 1.35 → 1.25 and 1.02 → 0.96
stellar host for host-dominated quasarsχ2ν 1.10; limits per fit 2.9 → 1.8; BIC better for 82%
Fe II shift tied to broad HβBIC better for 72%; the free shift ended at its limit in a quarter of the fits
no narrow Mg IIBIC better for 77%; parameters at a limit 1.1 → 0.3 per fit
two broad C IV componentsBIC better for 98%; χ2ν 1.76 → 1.52
broad-line test of classifytype 1 found in 76% of the quasars, 0.9% of the narrow-line galaxies

The scripts and reports are in calibration/ of the repository.
