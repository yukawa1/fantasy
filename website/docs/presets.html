FANTASY 2.0.0 / Modelling

Presets

Ready-made models for the common spectral windows and object classes. Every preset is an ordinary fa.Model: change it as you like.
presetrangecontent
hbeta4150–5550 Åpower law; Fe II (shift tied to broad Hβ); narrow and broad Hβ, Hγ; [O III] with an outflow wing; He II
halpha6200–6900 ÅHα, [N II], [S II], [O I], He I
optical3700–6900 Åall of the above, Balmer continuum, high-order Balmer lines; physical=True: one power law, stellar host, broad FWHM ≤ 15 000 km/s
mgii2650–3050 ÅMg II with UV Fe II and the Balmer continuum
civ1450–1720 ÅC IV with two broad components, He II 1640 tied to C IV
seyfert2, compositeopticalstellar populations with dust, weak scattered continuum, two narrow-line systems, outflow wing (seyfert2)
lineropticalold population with dust, one narrow-line system; broad=True for a LINER 1.9
starformingopticalyoung + old BC03 populations, Case B Balmer ratios with gas E(B−V), auroral lines
passiveopticalstellar populations with dust; weak lines with limited widths
tde, bffopticalblackbody + host, broad Balmer and He II with the Bowen lines; bff adds Fe II and coronal lines

Options

broad_components
1–3 broad components per line (core, wings, very broad).
outflow
a blueshifted wing on the forbidden lines.
host
a stellar host (True, a template set such as "bc03").
broad_profile
"lorentzian" or "stratified" broad lines.
coronal, oi6046, red
[Fe VII], [Fe X], [Fe XIV]; broad O I 6046; the range to 7500 Å.
feii, wave_range
(Mg II) the Fe II model and the window.
model = fa.presets.optical(broad_components=2, outflow=True, host=True)
models = fa.presets.variants("hbeta", host=(False, True))   # alternatives for fa.compare
fa.presets.available(spec)                                  # the presets whose window the spectrum covers
