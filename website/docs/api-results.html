FANTASY 2.1.0 / API reference

Results

FitResult.key_results()

The main numbers of the fit as a table (quantity, value, error, unit): for broad-line objects the FWHM and flux of the main broad line, the black-hole mass and the Balmer decrement; for narrow-line objects [OIII]5007 and the BPT ratios; the stellar velocity dispersion and host fraction when a stellar host is fitted.

FitResult.lines

Every fitted line with flux, FWHM, shift and equivalent width.

FitResult.line_limit(line, component=None, nsigma=3.0, level=0.95)

Upper limit on the flux of a line that is not detected.

With posterior samples (errors='mcmc') it is the one-sided level limit of the samples (upper_limit). Otherwise it is max(flux, 0) + nsigma * sigma_F, where sigma_F is the error of the flux of a line with the fitted width and shift, measured from the noise under it alone (the other parameters held at their values; errors scaled as in the fit): the flux a line of that shape would need to be seen at nsigma.

lineline name, e.g. '[OIII]5007'.
componentthe component (default: the first that has the line).
returnsdict with limit, method ('mcmc' or 'noise'), nsigma or level, sigma (sigma_F, for the noise method) and the fitted flux.

FitResult.upper_limit(name, level=0.95)

One-sided limit on a parameter from the posterior samples (errors='mcmc'), e.g. the flux of an undetected line. It assumes the flat priors within the parameter limits, so it depends on those limits.

FitResult.measure(line, components=None, moment_window=None)

Measure the intrinsic summed profile of the selected Lines components. Flux is the sum of normalized, integrated model line amplitudes. FWHM, shift and dispersion are in km/s; EW is relative to the AGN continuum in Å. A covered zero-flux line has undefined kinematics; a line without fitted pixels within one observed FWHM of its centre also has undefined flux and EW.

The centroid shift is the first moment within moment_window in km/s relative to the rest wavelength (default −40000 to +40000). Dispersion uses the same aperture. A contributing Lorentzian or Voigt damping wing has no finite intrinsic dispersion, so sigma is NaN unless you explicitly give a finite (vmin, vmax) within that range. Flux and FWHM are independent of that choice. fwhm_fragile marks a shoulder at half-maximum.

FitResult.bh_mass(line='Hb', components=None, recipe=None)

Single-epoch virial black-hole mass log10(M/Msun) from the FWHM of the (summed) broad line and a luminosity:

  • Hb: 'vp06' (default; Vestergaard & Peterson 2006, FWHM and L5100);

Dalla Bonta et al. 2020 (calibrated on SDSS-RM and the RM database, corrected for the Eddington-ratio bias) with the broad H-beta luminosity, 'db20' (FWHM) or 'db20_sigma' (line dispersion), or with L5100, 'db20_l5100' and 'db20_sigma_l5100'; or 'shen24' (Shen et al. 2024, SDSS-RM, L5100);

  • Ha: 'gh05' (default; Greene & Ho 2005) or 'rv15' (Reines & Volonteri

2015), both from the broad H-alpha luminosity, so no continuum (or host subtraction) is needed;

  • MgII2798: 'vo09' (default; Vestergaard & Osmer 2009), 'shen11'

(Shen et al. 2011) or 'shen24', all with L3000;

  • CIV1549: 'vp06' (default) or 'shen24', with L1350.

The error is statistical only; systematic_dex is the intrinsic scatter of the recipe (0.3-0.6 dex). A zero or uncovered broad line returns NaN. Dispersion-based recipes also return NaN for a line with damping wings; an aperture-dependent dispersion is not calibrated for these recipes.

FitResult.bh_masses()

The single-epoch black-hole mass from every recipe of :data:BH_RECIPES that the model allows (a broad H-beta, H-alpha, Mg II or C IV line), as a table: line, recipe key, reference, width (FWHM or sigma), luminosity used, log M_BH, statistical error and the intrinsic scatter of the recipe (dex).

FitResult.feii(component=None, band=None)

Fe II band flux, equivalent width and flux ratio to the total broad reference line. The optical default is 4434–4684 Å (air) divided by H-beta. The local UV template uses 2650–3050 Å and the extended UV template uses 2200–3090 Å, both divided by Mg II. Supply a rest-vacuum wavelength pair as band for a custom integral.

coverage is the fraction of the band covered by fitted pixels. Flux, EW and ratio are NaN below 95% coverage or outside the template's supported interval (3700–10900 Å optical, 2650–3050 Å local UV, 2200–3500 Å extended UV). A missing or undetected broad reference leaves the ratio undefined. ratio_label identifies the quantity in exports.

FitResult.balmer_decrement()

Broad H-alpha / H-beta flux ratio (all broad components of each line) with its error, or None if the model lacks either line. Unreddened broad-line AGN have about 3.06 with 0.03 dex scatter (Dong et al. 2008; 2.7-2.9 according to Gaskell 2017); values far outside 2.5-4 point to a decomposition problem (continuum vs. broad wings) or to reddening.

FitResult.undersampled(min_pixels=1.2)

Line components whose lines are narrower than the data can sample: the observed FWHM (the fitted width and the instrumental resolution in quadrature) is below min_pixels pixels. Profiles are averaged over the pixels, so the flux of such a line is kept, but a width below about a pixel barely changes the data (it is poorly measured) and the line can fit single noisy pixels. This happens when the instrumental resolution is unknown (spectrum.resolution = 0) and a narrow width ends at its lower limit. Returns a list of dicts: parameter, fwhm (the observed one, km/s), pixel (km/s), pixels (their ratio), resolution_known.

FitResult.host_properties(at=5100.0)

Stellar-population host: sigma* and v* (km/s), host fraction of the total model at wavelength at and light-weighted age (log Gyr) and metallicity [M/H] at the normalisation, with uncertainties.

FitResult.luminosity(at=5100.0)

log10 of lambda L_lambda (erg/s) of the AGN continuum, and its error (dex).

FitResult.continuum(at=5100.0)

AGN continuum flux density at a rest wavelength, with error.

FitResult.plot(ax=None, components=True, residuals=True, xlim=None, fig=None, width='double', group='auto', label_lines=False, title=None, style=True, **options)

Publication-quality plot of data, model, components and residuals; returns the figure. See fantasy_agn.plotting.plot_result for the options (colors, hide, data, model, legend, label_options).

width'single' (3.5 in) or 'double' (7.1 in) journal column, or inches.
group'auto' (default): components summed by kind, and the 1st, 2nd, ... broad component drawn too when a line has several; True: only the sums; False: every component separately.
label_linesname the strongest lines (True) or all fitted lines ('all').
xlimwavelength range to show, e.g. (4700, 5100) for a zoom.

FitResult.savefig(path, **plot_options)

Plot and save in one step; the format follows the extension (.pdf, .eps, .svg, .png). Fonts are embedded; raster formats use 300 dpi. Keyword arguments go to plot, e.g. width="single".

FitResult.save(prefix)

Write <prefix>_params.csv, _lines.csv, _model.csv and _summary.json.
