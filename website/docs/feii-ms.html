Fe II and the main sequence

The strength of Fe II relative to broad Hβ and the width of Hβ place a quasar on the quasar main sequence (Sulentic et al. 2000; Marziani et al. 2018).
RFe II = F(Fe II, 4434–4684 Å)F(Hβbroad)

The band is that of Boroson & Green (1992); for the UV template the band is 2200–3090 Å (Tsuzuki et al. 2006) and the reference line broad Mg II. Population A has FWHM(Hβ) < 4000 km/s; the spectral types A1–A4 are bins of 0.5 in RFe II, and B1, B1+, B1++ bins of 4000 km/s in FWHM.

r.feii()            # flux, EW and R_FeII with errors
r.main_sequence()   # R_FeII, FWHM(Hβ), population, spectral type
