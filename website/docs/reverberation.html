FANTASY 2.0.2 / Time domain and samples

Reverberation mapping

After a multi-epoch fit: the lag of a broad line behind the continuum, its width in the rms spectrum, and the reverberation mass. In the app: Results → Light curves & lags → Measure lag.
rm = me.reverberation("Hb")                                # continuum from the fitted 5100 Å
rm = me.reverberation("Hb", continuum=(mjd, flux, err))     # better: photometry
rm.table(); rm.lag_table(); rm.plot()
me.rms_spectrum("Hb")
Lag
interpolated cross-correlation (ICCF), centroid of r ≥ 0.8 rmax; errors by flux randomisation and random subset selection (Peterson et al. 1998, 2004). Also available: the von Neumann estimator (Chelouche et al. 2017), the z-transformed discrete correlation function (ZDCF; Alexander 1997) and a damped-random-walk model that uses the correlated errors of each epoch's fit. The ICCF is adopted; FANTASY warns when the methods disagree or a lag distribution has aliases.
Significance
rm.lag_table() has p_side, the share of the lag distribution (all samples, aliases included) on the side of zero where the lag is, and significance, its one-sided Gaussian equivalent (fa.rm.lag_significance(lag)). Below 2σ FANTASY warns that the lag is not significant, and below rmax = 0.5 that the light curves are weakly correlated.
rms spectrum
the broad line alone in every epoch, noise-corrected rms (Park et al. 2012), widths with bootstrap errors.
Mass
M = f cτ σline(rms)2/G with f = 4.47 (Woo et al. 2015); the 0.3 dex scatter of f is not in the error.

fa.rm.component_reverberation(me, "Hb", continuum=...) measures a lag and response for every component (continuum, each broad component, Fe II) from their fluxes and full error covariance over all epochs.

Check rmax (> 0.6–0.7 for a reliable lag) and the CCF: sparse continua diluted by host light, and long baselines with seasonal gaps, give spurious lags.
